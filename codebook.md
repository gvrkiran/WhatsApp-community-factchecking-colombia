# Interview codebook — community fact-checking on WhatsApp (Colombia, August–September 2025)

**Material.** Nine semi-structured interviews (20–30 min, Zoom, Spanish) with study participants P01–P09, conducted 28 Aug–10 Sep 2025 by the first author. Eight were audio-recorded and transcribed; the recording of P01 failed and the interview is represented by the interviewer's notes written immediately afterwards. Structured English notes per interview-guide section exist for all nine.

**Participants.** P01 (accuracy 0.80), P02 (0.68), P03 (0.53), P04 (0.69), P05 (0.53), P06 (0.36), P07 (0.70), P08 (0.78), P09 (0.73). Rows P01–P09 of the demographics table in the paper. P09 was living in Ecuador during the study.

**Procedure.** Reflexive thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke 2006, 2019). Pass 1: interview-guide sections used as an organizing frame (motivation; evaluation process; reaction to the community verdict; usability and a future system). Pass 2: open coding of transcripts and notes into 30 codes. Pass 3: codes grouped into four themes; code definitions tightened; every interview re-read against the final codebook. Counts below are the number of interviewees whose account instantiates a code; a code can be assigned only when the participant said it (not when the interviewer suggested it and the participant merely assented). Coding was done by one coder; no inter-rater reliability statistic is reported (McDonald, Schoenebeck & Forte 2019); the codebook and participant-level assignments are provided so readers can audit the counts. Quotes are translated from Spanish by the coder; the Spanish is given.

## Theme 1 — How participants decided whether an item was true (10 codes)

| Code | Definition | Participants | n |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 Prior knowledge and lived experience | Judgment grounded in what the participant already knew: professional training, local knowledge, having seen the item before | P01, P03, P05, P07, P08, P09 | 6 |
| H2 Local social-evidence test | Treating the absence of expected offline corroboration (worried families, news, posters) as proof that a local alarm is false | P07 | 1 |
| H3 Surface and production cues | Judging by how the item looks: production quality, editing, "professional" feel; realistic-looking video | P03, P05, P09 | 3 |
| H4 Unarticulated intuition | Could not name a reason beyond the item seeming suspicious | P02 | 1 |
| H5 Political stance and propaganda-as-falsity | Evaluating political items through one's own position; classifying any content perceived as partisan propaganda as false | P03, P04, P07, P09 | 4 |
| H6 External search | Looking the claim up on Google or in news before answering (P04 and P09 once only) | P03, P04, P05, P06, P08, P09 | 6 |
| H7 Social consultation | Asking or debating with family, partner, colleagues, classmates before answering | P01, P03, P06, P08, P09 | 5 |
| H8 Staying inside WhatsApp | Deliberately not leaving the chat to search, to avoid forgetting the task or losing the notification | P01, P07 | 2 |
| H9 Shortcuts in responding | Skimming long texts; balancing answers across true and false; guessing "true" when media failed to load so as not to lose the incentive | P03, P04, P07 | 3 |
| H10 Format-dependent handling | Videos deferred to a quiet moment at home; texts answered anywhere; long chains left to "sit" | P04, P06, P07, P09 | 4 |

Quotes.
- H1/H2 (P07): "cuando algo así pasa, pues uno ve... vos ves a las familias preocupadas, vos conocés a los familiares... ¿cómo se están robando niños y uno no sabe ni siquiera quién es el niño que robaron?" — "When something like that happens, you see it... you see the families worried, you know the relatives... how can children be being stolen and nobody even knows which child was stolen?"
- H1 (P05): "la mayoría de las preguntas eran cosas que mayormente sabía cómo responder" — "Most of the questions were things I mostly knew how to answer."
- H3 (P05): "se nota por la calidad de tiempo y la producción y cómo lo realizan... esto parece más que una publicidad de algún producto" — "You can tell from the quality, the time and production and how they make it... this looks more like an advertisement for a product."
- H3 (P09, on AI-generated videos): "esto se lo cree mi mamá" — "My mother would believe this."
- H4 (P02): "a mí me llevaba a pensar que era como... ya, no decía falso, no sé" — "It made me think it was, well... I don't know."
- H5 (P04): "mensajes de información falsa que hacían de lado izquierdo o lado derecho... así trataba de deducir de que pues era falso" — "False-information messages made by the left or the right... that is how I deduced it was false."
- H5 (P07): "yo ya tengo una posición política, entonces, ya" — "I already have a political position, so that's that."
- H6 (P03): "yo me iba a Google y yo, ah, esto sí salió, entonces sí fue verdad" — "I went to Google and, ah, this did come out, so it was true."
- H7 (P03, on a scam-warning chain): "lo leí incluso delante de mi esposo... él dijo, sí eso es verdad, hágale, conteste que es verdad" — "I even read it in front of my husband... he said, yes that's true, go on, answer that it's true." (The item was false.)
- H7 (P06): "era como para debatir y ahí entré yo en una razón y poder responder" — "It was to debate, and from that I reached a reason and could answer."
- H8 (P01, notes): she did not search because she feared that if she left WhatsApp she would forget to come back and answer.
- H8 (P07): "si yo veo el mensaje, paila, no respondía" — "If I open the message, that's it, I wouldn't answer" (so she kept videos unopened as a reminder).
- H9 (P04): "la mayoría de las veces creía que todo era falso, trataba como que de equilibrar las respuestas" — "Most of the time I thought everything was false; I tried to sort of balance the answers."
- H9 (P03): "no puedo perder mis 50 mil pesos. Voy a contestar verdadero" — "I can't lose my 50,000 pesos. I'll answer true."
- H9 (P07): "Yo leo un poquito de la parte de arriba, luego un poquito de la parte central y al final" — "I read a bit of the top, then a bit of the middle and the end."
- H10 (P06): "si era una pregunta de leer, siempre en clase va a haber tiempo, pero ya cuando es video, esperaba llegar a la casa" — "If it was something to read there is always time in class, but when it's a video I waited to get home."

## Theme 2 — Reactions to the community verdict (7 codes)

| Code | Definition | Participants | n |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1 Agreement as confirmation | When the majority matched their answer they felt confirmed and moved on | P01, P02, P04, P05, P06, P09 | 6 |
| V2 Disagreement as self-doubt or a cue to re-check | Re-read the item, wondered whether they were wrong, or resolved to think harder next time | P02, P04, P05, P06, P08 | 5 |
| V3 Disagreement attributed to the community | Explained disagreement by others' lack of knowledge, age, or political leaning; used the verdict to profile the community | P01, P03, P07, P09 | 4 |
| V4 Verdict given little attention | Reported barely looking at, or not using, the community results | P03, P07, P09 | 3 |
| V5 Game framing | Described agreeing with the majority as "winning" and disagreeing as "losing" | P04, P06 | 2 |
| V6 Unclear meaning of the message | Did not understand what the percentages meant or who "the community" was | P08 | 1 |
| V7 Trust in the aggregate conditional on who is in it | Would trust the community only if it were heterogeneous; or trusted it specifically on unfamiliar topics | P05 (trusted), P07, P09 (conditional) | 3 |

Quotes.
- V1 (P09): "había una sensación de que era la verdadera, o sea, de que sí se estaba construyendo como un consenso" — "There was a feeling that it was the true one, that a consensus was being built."
- V2 (P02): "yo me ponía a leer nuevamente para ver si era que... ¿qué había pasado?" — "I would read it again to see whether... what had happened?"
- V2 (P08): "¿estará buena o estaré equivocada?" — "Is it right, or am I wrong?"
- V2 (P05): "no sabía que era de la otra opción... ¿o de pronto no estaba bien informado?" — "I didn't know it was the other option... or maybe I wasn't well informed?"
- V3 (P03): "hay más petristas que uribistas... la mayoría jóvenes, pelados" — "There are more Petro supporters than Uribe supporters... mostly young people, kids."
- V3 (P09): "deben ser personas de centro derecha... esa comunidad no me representa" — "They must be centre-right people... that community does not represent me."
- V3 (P07): "muchas de esas opiniones tal vez eran muy personales... dependiendo de la edad" — "Many of those opinions were probably very personal... depending on age."
- V4 (P07): "yo sabía lo que había puesto y creía en lo que había puesto y ya" — "I knew what I had put and I believed in what I had put, and that was it."
- V4 (P09): "no usaba esos datos para tomar la siguiente decisión... siempre estuve como en la mía" — "I didn't use those data to make the next decision... I always stuck to my own view."
- V5 (P06): "Yo decía: 'Ey, voy bien', o cuando: 'Mierda, la perdí'" — "I'd say 'Hey, I'm doing well', or 'Damn, I lost that one'."
- V5 (P04): "era como que, ay, la gané, no la perdí" — "It was like, oh, I won it, I didn't lose it."
- V6 (P08): "decía como que el 80% verdadero y el 20% falso. O sea, no entendí... ¿estaba bien la respuesta o la mayoría pensamos esto?" — "It said something like 80% true and 20% false. I didn't understand... was the answer right, or is it that most of us thought this?"
- V7 (P09): "tener la certeza de que la comunidad está compuesta muy heterogéneamente... en grados etarios, en escolaridad, en nivel profesional" — "To be certain the community is very heterogeneous... in age, schooling, professional level."

## Theme 3 — Motivation and how the task fit into daily life (6 codes)

| Code | Definition | Participants | n |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 Payment | Named the incentive as a reason to keep responding (P06: initially; P07: main reason) | P01, P03, P04, P05, P06, P07 | 6 |
| M2 Local relevance and realism of the content | Valued that items came from their own environment and reflected "what is really happening" | P01, P03, P04, P05, P06 | 5 |
| M3 Learning, curiosity, interest | Found the items interesting; learned about the country; curious about the next item | P01, P02, P05, P06, P08, P09 | 6 |
| M4 Commitment to the researcher | Felt an obligation to the interviewer and the study | P01 | 1 |
| M5a Pre-existing critical stance | Already distrusted circulating content before the study | P01, P05, P07 | 3 |
| M5b Study prompted more checking | Said the true/false task led them to investigate or question content they previously took mechanically | P08, P09 | 2 |
| M6 Fitting the task into routines | Answered in downtime, after class or work, or immediately to avoid forgetting | P01, P02, P03, P04, P05, P06, P07, P08, P09 | 9 |

Quotes.
- M1 (P07): "si no respondía no me iban a pagar el total" — "If I didn't respond they wouldn't pay me the full amount."
- M1 (P05): "sería mentira decirte que... bueno, sí, el dinero era un incentivo, pero igual para mí la actividad me pareció como dinámica" — "It would be a lie to tell you... well, yes, the money was an incentive, but the activity also seemed dynamic to me."
- M1/M3 (P06): "a uno lo que lo motivó fue que dijeron del pago... [luego] me preocupé más en la sociedad que en mí mismo" — "What motivated me was that they mentioned the payment... [later] I cared more about society than about myself."
- M2 (P03): "Todo fue en base a lo que realmente está sucediendo, fue real" — "Everything was based on what is really happening, it was real."
- M2 (P01, notes): she was surprised to receive a news item about her own town from a study run by a US university.
- M3 (P02): "aprendí bastante algunas cosas que no sabía" — "I learned quite a lot of things I didn't know."
- M3 (P09): "le daba a uno interés de decir, ¿qué vendrá después?" — "It made you curious: what will come next?"
- M5b (P09): "creo que era muy mecánico... no lo había cuestionado" — "I think it was very mechanical... I hadn't questioned it."
- M6 (P01, notes): answered in "tiempos muertos" (dead time) while cooking or in waiting rooms.

## Theme 4 — Conditions for a real system (8 codes)

| Code | Definition | Participants | n |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 Trust and scam fear as the entry barrier | Unknown numbers are assumed to be scams; participation depended on a known contact vouching, or would require a trusted institution (the State, WhatsApp itself) | P01, P07 | 2 |
| S2 Representativeness as a condition of legitimacy | Would trust or join only if the community were demonstrably heterogeneous | P07, P09 | 2 |
| S3 Purpose and tangible action | Would not contribute opinions without a visible outcome; wants the "why" communicated as a common good | P03, P06, P07 | 3 |
| S4 Cadence and deadlines | Two items a day felt like a lot for some; one or two per day with spacing, and explicit response windows, suggested | P03, P04, P05, P06, P08, P09 | 6 |
| S5 WhatsApp channel is easy | Answering A/B in WhatsApp is easy for everyone, easier than forms; simplicity also invites postponing | P02, P05, P06, P07, P09 | 5 |
| S6 Engagement design | Make messages more attractive or attention-grabbing; allow opinions and suggestions beyond true/false | P03, P08, P09 | 3 |
| S7 Technical failure | A video did not load; participant guessed to protect the incentive | P03 | 1 |
| S8 WhatsApp lacks a community-warning layer | Contrasted Facebook's community warnings with their absence on WhatsApp | P05 | 1 |

Quotes.
- S1 (P01, notes): she joined because a person she trusted knew the experimenter and said he was "de fiar" (trustworthy); "lo principal es construir y garantizar la confianza" — "the main thing is to build and guarantee trust."
- S1 (P07): "le daba miedo que fuera una estafa... si vos ves que es una entidad confiable, por ejemplo, que está respaldado por el Estado... entonces, listo, ayudo" — "She was afraid it was a scam... if you see it's a trustworthy entity, for example backed by the State... then fine, I'll help."
- S3 (P03): "no solamente es enviar información... palabras que se la lleva el viento. Si es con el objetivo realmente de hacer algo... perfecto" — "Not just sending information... words the wind carries away. If the aim is really to do something... perfect."
- S4 (P09): "uno o dos diarios... [si no] es como cuando te llaman así del banco" — "One or two a day... [otherwise] it's like when the bank calls you." Also: "tienes 24 horas, tienes 8 horas... uno ya se puede proyectar" — "You have 24 hours, you have 8 hours... then you can plan."
- S4 (P08): "uno al día estaría bien. O si fuesen dos, entonces con bastante intervalo de tiempo" — "One a day would be fine. Or if two, with a good interval between them."
- S5 (P07): "era simplemente colocar A o B... una red social que prácticamente todo el mundo utiliza" — "It was simply putting A or B... a network that practically everyone uses."
- S6 (P08): "a nosotros en las redes nos llama mucho la atención lo que sea bonito, lo que se vea atractivo o lo que genere morbo" — "On social networks what catches our attention is what is pretty, attractive, or generates morbid curiosity."
- S8 (P05): "WhatsApp a veces carece de esa pequeña parte que tiene Facebook y hay veces que la gente se cree cadenas" — "WhatsApp sometimes lacks that small part Facebook has, and sometimes people believe chain messages."
